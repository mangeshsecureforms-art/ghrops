Candidate Privacy Notice

For job applicants and candidates · Last updated 26 August 2026

If you're reading this, chances are you've applied for a job somewhere, and that organisation happens to use Apptomax Recruit to manage the process. Worth saying clearly: we're the technology, not your employer. The company hiring you calls the shots on how your information is used.

ℹ️
The recruiting organisation using Apptomax Recruit decides why your information is collected and how long it's kept. For questions about a specific application, they're the ones to ask.

1What your candidate information might include

NameContact information ResumeEducation Employment historyInterview information Interview feedbackIdentity documents Other documents requested by the recruiting organisation

2What it's typically used for

Recruiting teams generally use your information through the platform for things like:

  • Reviewing and shortlisting applications
  • Scheduling and managing interviews
  • Staying in touch with you throughout the process
  • Verifying information or documents you've submitted
  • Collecting documents needed for hiring
  • Getting you set up once you've accepted an offer

3Who's actually in control

The organisation you applied to is the one making the decisions about your data inside the platform. We just process it on their behalf.

4Your options

If you're wondering why your information was collected, how long it'll be kept, or want to access, correct or delete it start with the recruiting organisation. They hold the answers, because they're the ones who made the underlying decisions.
