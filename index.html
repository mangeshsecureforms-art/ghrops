FAQs

Before you book the demo.

Everything HR teams usually ask us first.

Do we need a separate Microsoft license? +
No. The full 8-stage pipeline runs on the Microsoft 365 subscription you already have — no extra per-user platform fees.
How many interview rounds can we set up? +
Up to five per position, each with its own name and interviewer — the app generates a private link for every round automatically.
Do interviewers need to log into the app? +
No. They get a secure link that opens a simple page for shortlisting, rejecting or leaving comments — no login required.
How is the offer letter generated? +
HR enters the candidate's details and Annual CTC. Every salary component is calculated automatically and a formatted offer letter is ready to send.
Where do joining documents get stored? +
Every upload is saved straight to your organisation's SharePoint, so access stays governed by your own Microsoft 365 permissions.
How long until we're live? +
Typically 1–2 weeks, covering setup, branding, position configuration and a short training session for your HR team.
